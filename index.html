👨‍🎓About me

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Large-Scale Data & Systems (LSDS) group at Imperial College London, where I work on cloud networking with Dr. Marios Kogias. I received my Ph.D. from the University of Edinburgh in 2025, advised by Prof. Paul Patras, and my M.Sc. from Central South University, advised by Prof. Jiawei Huang.

My research focuses on sketches and stream analytics, data-intensive systems, and programmable data planes. Modern systems need answers from data streams that arrive faster than exact state can be kept: which items are heavy, which keep recurring, which stay stable. I design sketches, compact data structures with provable error bounds, to answer such questions, and build them into systems that run at line rate on multi-core CPUs, programmable switches, and FPGAs. I have also worked on multimedia, designing learning-based adaptive bitrate algorithms for video streaming, and on data center networks, including load balancing and traffic scheduling.

As first author, I have published at ACM SIGMOD, IEEE ICDE, ACM SIGKDD, and ACM WWW, and received the ACM WWW 2024 Best Student Paper Award.
